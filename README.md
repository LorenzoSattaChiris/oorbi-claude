# Oorbi

Oorbi helps you get better answers from AI and keep what works.

- **Improve a prompt.** Ask "Oorbi, improve this prompt" before you send something. The AI you are using does the improving itself, keeping your meaning, facts and language. Your prompt is not sent to Oorbital.
- **Save a Move.** Turn a prompt that worked into a reusable Move, with inputs you fill each time. You see the exact draft and the library it goes to before anything is saved. A new Move is private to you.
- **Find and use your Moves.** Search your own Moves, the ones colleagues shared with you, your company's and Oorbital's starters. Use one here, or get it ready to send. Your company decides whether its Moves can be opened in this AI.

The same Moves are in Oorbi in Chrome and the Oorbital Console.

## What you need

Improving a prompt works without an account. Finding and saving Moves needs an Oorbital account: connect Oorbi when your AI asks, and sign in to Oorbital.

## What is sent where

| You do | Oorbital receives |
|---|---|
| Improve a prompt | Nothing |
| Find a Move | The words you search for |
| Open a Move | Your request for that Move; the Move's words come back to this AI |
| Fill a Move's inputs | Nothing. Your answers stay in this conversation |
| Save or update a Move | Its title, summary and instruction |

Oorbi never reads your conversation history, and opening or using a Move here is not counted as a use.
